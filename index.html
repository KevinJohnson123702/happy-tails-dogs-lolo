Pet care tips

Little things that help

Simple preparation can make a visit easier for both pets and people.

🥣

Keep supplies together

Put food, treats, leashes, litter supplies, and other essentials somewhere easy to find.

🔑

Plan access ahead

Make sure the agreed entry method works before the visit, especially when you're away.

📝

Share the routine

Tell us about feeding times, favorite activities, medications, or behavior notes that matter.

Before the visit

A few important notes

Clear expectations help keep pets, homes, and visits safe.

🐾 Meet the pet first

For a new pet or unfamiliar situation, a short meet-and-greet can help everyone understand the routine.

🔐 Safe access

Only use the agreed way to enter the home. Please share any access instructions before the visit.

📞 Changes happen

If plans change, contact Happy Tails as soon as possible so the visit can be discussed.

Services can be declined or stopped when an animal or environment cannot be handled safely.
